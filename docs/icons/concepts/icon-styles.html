Polymine icon styles

Seven different drawing styles, next to the current icon on the left. Each is shown at 64, 32 and 16 px on light and dark. Pick a style, then we design properly inside it.

1 Pastel

Soft gradient and a friendly round mine with a face. Cozy, casual, kind to new players.

2 Hand-drawn

Graph paper and pen: a sketched corner of a board, as if worked out on paper.

3 Realistic

A studded metal mine on a brushed tile, with light and shadow. Old-school skeuomorphic.

4 iOS

An Apple squircle with a bright gradient and a white glyph grid, one flagged cell in red.

5 Android

Material You: tonal purples in a circle, bold flat shapes, an adaptive-icon silhouette.

6 Pixel art

Windows 95 grey and a 1-bit mine. Pure nostalgia for where FastMines came from.

7 Neon

A glowing hex and mine on night purple, in the spirit of the Midnight theme.
