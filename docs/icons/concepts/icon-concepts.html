Polymine icon concepts

Four directions in the Graphite palette, next to the current icon on the left. Each is shown at 64, 32 and 16 px, the sizes of the Dock, taskbar and tabs. Pick one or two to refine.

1 Pupil

An eye whose pupil is a mine. The app already uses eyes for wins and losses.

2 Rosette

A cutout of squares, triangles and hexagons, one cell opened to a 2 and one flagged: minesweeper on many shapes.

3 Soft hole

A field of closed cells with a soft hole in the middle and a single mine where you can't see it.

4 Flag tile

One bevelled closed cell with a flag: the most classic, and the clearest at 16 px.
